# Creative Concept Method — audience- & scenario-anchored

The team creates the idea; the AI helps sharpen it. A great concept is **anchored to a specific audience and a specific moment** — it is never a generic slogan floating in space.

## 0. The creative brief (anchor before ideating)

Before diverging, lock the anchor. Fill ONE line:

> For **[primary segment]** , whose **[emotional job]** , at **[moment of truth]** , our campaign will **[mechanic]** — so they **[desired belief/action]** .

This forces every idea to answer *who*, *what job*, *when/where*. The HMW is derived from it:

> "How might we help **[segment]** **[job]** at **[moment]** ?"

## 1. The scenario canvas (time / place / event)

List **3–5 campaign moments** where the brand can intercept the audience — the specific times, places, and events where the decision is made. Pick the 1–2 highest-leverage ones as the creative spine.

| # | Moment | Time | Place | Event |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ticket-sale day | 18 months out | phone / creator feed | creator film drop |
| 2 | the journey | travel window | airport stopover | crew meet-up |
| 3 | match day | Games week | the stands | away-section roar |

- Quest A examples: team-qualification announcement · ticket-sale day · the Qatar Airways stopover · Ramadan/school-holiday window · medal moments.
- Quest B examples: a panda's birthday livestream · World Panda Day · holiday gifting · the 2 a.m. viral clip.

## 2. What a finished concept contains

| Element | A — campaign | B — membership |
|---|---|---|
| Name | campaign name | program name |
| Slogan | rallying line | member promise line |
| Proposition | why fans should act now | why fans should join |
| Hero idea | the one memorable mechanic | the membership hook |
| Offer | ticket/experience bundle | founding-member offer (tiers, price, benefits) |
| **Scenario** | the moment(s) it intercepts | the moment(s) it intercepts |

## 3. How to get there (facilitated)

1. **Anchor** — fill the creative brief (audience + job + moment).
2. **Scenario canvas** — list 3–5 moments; pick the spine.
3. **Frame** — derive the HMW from the anchor.
4. **Diverge** — silent brainstorm → round-robin → affinity cluster (aim for 10–20 ideas).
5. **Converge** — dot-vote; keep the top 2–3.
6. **Deepen** — 1-2-4-All on the winner; force a name + slogan + the moment it owns.
7. **Decide** — the team locks the winner.

## 4. Quality bar

- **Anchored**: name the segment + the moment it serves (not "everyone, always").
- **Specific & ownable**: could a rival run it unchanged? If yes, sharpen it.
- **Human**: it speaks to a real emotional job.
- **Feasible under MVP budget**: runnable in 3 months in 2 markets.
- **On-brand**: no over-commercialization for the panda (conservation-first).

## 5. Adding the marketing layer (AI, after the team decides)

- Positioning statement + 4Ps (see `marketing-plan.md`).
- For B: membership tiers + founding offer.

## 6. Anti-patterns

- A "big idea" with no audience anchor or no moment (the root cause of thin concepts).
- The AI choosing the idea.
- "Big idea" that is actually a feature list.
- A slogan with no proposition behind it.
- Forcing a single idea for a group of 10 — always diverge first.
