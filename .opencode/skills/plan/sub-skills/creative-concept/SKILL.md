# Creative Concept — anchor, diverge, converge

Develop the campaign's big idea. **Anchor first** (audience + moment), then **diverge** (creative methods → raw ideas), then **converge** (the ~6 candidates → one complete campaign, in the plan stage).

> A thin idea is an unanchored idea. Every idea must answer *who, what job, when/where* — never a generic slogan floating in space. And in the plan stage, every idea must **carry the team's concept keywords** — they are the creative driver, not decoration.

## Inputs

- **The team's concept keywords (1–2)** — supplied at the opening of the plan stage; the campaign must visibly reflect them.
- The **selected key insights** (2–3) from the insight stage — each fuses a **segment × moment × trend**.
- The segment's **`channels`** field and the insight's **trend** (the raw material for the channel).
- The **Quest's How Might We (HMW)**.
- The org profile + audience map (for grounding).

## Phase 1 — Anchor (before any ideation)

**1a. The creative brief.** Lock the anchor in ONE line — the full **campaign kernel** (`audience · moment · trend · channel · concept`), with the concept keywords folded into the **mechanic/desire**:

> For **[primary segment]**, whose **[emotional job]**, at **[moment of truth]**, riding **[trend]**, our campaign intercepts via **[channel]** with **[mechanic — expressing the keywords]** — so they **[desired belief/action]**.

**1b. Scenario canvas.** List **3–5 campaign moments** (time · place · event) where the brand intercepts the audience; pick the 1–2 highest-leverage as the creative spine.

| Moment | Time | Place | Event |
|---|---|---|---|
| sale / drop day | 18 months out | phone / creator feed | creator film drop |
| the journey | travel window | a stopover | crew meet-up |
| event day | the event window | the venue | the shared moment |

- Draw the moments from the card: a qualification/launch news · a sale/drop day · a journey/stopover · a seasonal window · an event moment · a birthday livestream · a holiday gifting window · a viral clip.

## Phase 2 — Diverge (HMW + creative-thinking methods)

1. **Frame** — turn the anchor into ONE "How might we…" question that **includes the concept keywords** (e.g. *How might we make first-timers feel national pride at the ticket-drop moment?*).
2. **Diverge** — run several methods to force non-obvious ideas (see `references/creative-methods.md`), using the keywords as a lens:
   - SCAMPER · Analogies · Reverse/bad ideas · Mash-ups · Crazy 8 · "What would [brand] do" · Brainwriting (6-3-5).

## Phase 3 — Converge → ~6 ideas → one campaign

1. Cluster the raw ideas and distil to **~6 candidates** (a numbered list).
2. Each idea: **name · one-line · the insight it answers · the mechanic · the keyword(s) it expresses**.
3. **In the plan stage the AI converges these into ONE complete campaign built directly from the team's keywords** — the team does **not** pick an idea or an A/B; its only input was the concept keywords themselves (see `plan` → *One input only*). Standalone use: if this sub-skill is invoked on its own, the team may pick/combine 2–3.

> The stage's only team input is the concept keywords (1–2), given up front — the campaign is then generated directly from them.

## What a finished concept contains

| Element | Campaign | Membership |
|---|---|---|
| Name | campaign name | program name |
| Slogan | rallying line | member promise line |
| Proposition | why fans should act now | why fans should join |
| Hero idea | the one memorable mechanic | the membership hook |
| Offer | ticket/experience bundle | founding-member offer (tiers, price, benefits) |
| Scenario | the moment(s) it intercepts | the moment(s) it intercepts |
| Channel | the primary channel it intercepts through | the primary channel it intercepts through |

## Quality bar

- **Anchored** — names the segment + the moment it serves.
- **Keyword-aligned** — a reader can see the team's 1–2 concept keywords in the name, mechanic, or proposition.
- **Channeled** — names the media channel it intercepts through (from the segment's `channels` + the trend).
- **Specific & ownable** — a rival couldn't run it unchanged.
- **Human** — speaks to a real emotional job.
- **Feasible under the MVP budget** — runnable in 3 months in 2 markets.
- **On-brand** — cause-first where the card's subject is an IP / conservation asset.

## Rules & anti-patterns

- Ideas grounded in the selected insights (not random); 6–8 max, distinct.
- Ideas must trace to the team's concept keywords; the AI converges, and the campaign is generated directly from the keywords.
- ❌ A "big idea" with no audience anchor or no moment.
- ❌ A "big idea" with no channel (where it reaches the audience).
- ❌ A "big idea" that ignores the team's concept keywords.
- ❌ A "big idea" that is actually a feature list.
- ❌ A slogan with no proposition behind it.
- ❌ Forcing one idea on a group of 10 — always diverge first.
