# Creative-Thinking Methods (for idea divergence)

Use several of these to force non-obvious campaign ideas. Run them **on the selected insights**, not in the abstract.

---

## 1. HMW — "How Might We"

Reframe a tension into a single, solvable, opportunity-framed question.

- One sentence, starts "How might we…", positive frame, not too broad ("make money") or too narrow ("add a red button").
- Derived from the selected insight(s).

Example: *"How might we help [audience] feel [emotion / belonging] at [moment of truth], at the moment [trigger]?"*

## 2. SCAMPER

Ask each verb against the current offer / behavior:

- **S**ubstitute — swap a component (channel, price, audience, format).
- **C**ombine — merge two offers/mechanics (ticket + flight + stay).
- **A**dapt — borrow a mechanic that works elsewhere.
- **M**odify / Magnify — amplify a feature or emotion.
- **P**ut to another use — repurpose an asset (IP, venue, mascot).
- **E**liminate — remove a friction or requirement.
- **R**everse / Rearrange — flip the order, the moment, the audience.

## 3. Analogies — borrow from another category

"How would [category] solve this?" then map it back.

- Music fandom (fan clubs, tiered membership, "stan" culture)
- Gaming (seasons, battle passes, guilds, leaderboards)
- Travel (bundles, loyalty tiers, stopover packages)
- Gifting / collectibles (limited drops, unboxing, adoption)

## 4. Reverse / bad ideas

1. List deliberately **bad** ideas ("make it slower", "charge more for less").
2. Invert each into a real idea.
   - Bad: "make fans wait months" → Real: a countdown/roadmap that builds anticipation.

## 5. Mash-ups

Combine two unrelated concepts into one mechanic.

- "Ticket" + "passport" = a stamped fan passport across venues.
- "Watch party" + "airline" = a flying watch party / chartered fan flight.

## 6. Crazy 8

Each person writes **8 one-line ideas in 8 minutes** (no judging). Force quantity; the best often come at #6–#8.

## 7. "What would [brand] do"

Run the problem through a familiar brand's lens, then translate:
- "What would **Nike** do?" (own a moment / a feeling)
- "What would **Disney** do?" (a story + a belonging world)
- "What would **Spotify** do?" (personalized, shareable identity)

## 8. Brainwriting (6-3-5)

6 people × 3 ideas each, pass the sheet 5 times, build on others' ideas. Good for a group of ~10: two rounds.

---

## Converging to ~6 ideas

After running 2–4 methods, cluster and distil to **~6**:
- Each idea = **name · one-line · insight it answers · mechanic**.
- Distinct, nameable, grounded in the insight.
- Present as a numbered list; in the plan stage the AI converges these into **ONE complete campaign built directly from the team's concept keywords** (not an idea menu, and no A/B).
