What it is

Part of the Ascentium AI Transformation (Inspire) program. For one afternoon, you will work in a cross-BU guild and run the full AI co-creation loop end to end — from first insight to a finished, pitchable plan. There is no slide marathon and no theory: you are hands-on from the first minute.

What you will do

  • Take on a real marketing case — the brief is revealed on the day.
  • Build the solution with a pre-loaded AI toolkit, guided step by step.
  • Shape it into a short pitch: the insight, the plan, and the results it expects.
  • Present in a friendly head-to-head duel, then vote for the winners.
