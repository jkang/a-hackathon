Client · Chengdu Research Base of Giant Panda Breeding — Global Brand & IP Division
The Asset · Chengdu Panda Base
  • 200+ giant pandas — world’s largest conservation & breeding population; ¥55 entry

The Gap

  • Capacity ceiling: on-site growth is capped; the next frontier must be virtual + licensed
The Proof · Panda Economics

The Arena: Showcase Duels

same-quest pairs · everyone votes

🎲Draw Lots First

8 guilds draw lots for pick order AND duel slots: pick Quest A/B in order (4 guilds per quest, first-come-first-served; once A is full, the rest take B), then same-quest guilds pair up (D1–D4). Brackets published in advance for targeted prep.

⏱️20′ per Duel

Each duel (4×20′): Lucky Draw 1′ → two 5′ pitches → 2′ cross-questioning → 1′ rally → live vote 2′. Other 6 guilds + the host vote on stage.

🏅Winners & Awards

Every duel win builds your tally; total votes across all duels crown the Champion and Runner-up, and every other team earns a playful honor.

🎰

Lucky Draw · Random Element Challenge — inject random creativity into every pitch

Before stepping up, each guild draws 1 random “lucky element” — a keyword, gesture, prop or catchphrase — and must weave it naturally into the 5′ pitch. The host rules on the spot whether it landed. It doesn't move the duel score, but it heavily swings the Crowd Favorite race.
