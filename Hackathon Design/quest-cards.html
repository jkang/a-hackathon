Client · OCA — Doha 2030 Ticketing & Fan Engagement Unit

01 Scout Report · As of 2026-09

Gold Standard · Hangzhou 2023
  • 3.05M tickets sold · ¥610M ticket revenue
  • 92% attendance; hot sessions >90%
  • Market development ¥5.32B total: sponsorship ¥4.42B + licensed merch ¥760M (17 categories, ~1,500 SKUs; mascot “江南忆” = 70% of merch sales)
Cautionary Tale · Nagoya 2026
  • Games ongoing: Sept 19 – Oct 4, 2026; 41 sports
  • Plan: 2.75M tickets → only ~605K sold at T-14 days (22%); <30% by late Aug
  • Budget ballooned ¥85B → ¥240B; opening-ceremony tickets ¥7,500–60,000
  • China visa fee +~5× since Jul 2026; sponsors only Japanese (Toyota, Chubu Electric)
  • Structural mismatch: kabaddi / sepak takraw / badminton sold out — football & baseball seats empty
Your Arena · Doha 2030
  • 21st Asian Games, Nov 2030 · 54 sports at 39 venues
  • Khalifa Int’l Stadium hosts the Opening; billiards returns after 20 yrs; equestrian archery newly added
  • Qatar National Vision 2030: tourism diversification is a national KPI

Insight: Nagoya’s lesson — demand does not appear by itself; it must be manufactured market by market. Hangzhou proved a 92%-full stadium is possible. Doha’s edge: visa-free entry, a world-class stopover hub (Qatar Airways / Hamad Int’l), and the Games’ own home continent. Mission: make Asia’s away crowd Doha’s biggest in Asian Games history.

PANDA GOES GLOBAL — From Zoo Visit to World Membership

Client · Chengdu Research Base of Giant Panda Breeding — Global Brand & IP Division

01 Scout Report · As of 2026-09

The Asset · Chengdu Panda Base
  • 200+ giant pandas — world’s largest conservation & breeding population; ¥55 entry
  • Weekday 15–20K visitors/day; summer 30–42K; weekend peaks 45K (capacity ceiling)
  • 2024 Spring Festival: 442K visitors in 7 days; May Day 2024: 264K in 3 days — tickets “gone in seconds”
  • Superstar pandas: “He Hua 花花” a national icon; “Fu Bao 福宝” returned from Korea Apr 2024, viral worldwide
The Gap
  • Fame is global, monetization is local: no membership program, no global store, no English-first content engine
  • International visitors ~5% of total footfall — fans in Jakarta / Riyadh / São Paulo can watch but can’t “belong”
  • Capacity ceiling: on-site growth is capped; the next frontier must be virtual + licensed
The Proof · Panda Economics
  • 487K international visitors in 2025 (Jan 1–Nov 13), +70.7% YoY — Xinhua
  • One visit to see 花花 ≈ ¥1,500 of city-wide spend (Qunar estimate)
  • Xiang Xiang (Japan): ~¥2.7B economic contribution in <6 years; Fu Bao merch: 2.7M units in Korea, +60%
Insight: The pandas are already Earth’s most beloved IP — but the Base captures almost none of that love beyond the ¥55 gate. Japan and Korea proved a single panda can mint billions in merch and tourism. Mission: turn “the world’s most famous zoo” into a global membership brand.
