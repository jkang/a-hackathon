FIRST WHISTLE — manufacture demand where none exists

OCA — Doha 2030 Ticketing & Fan Engagement Unit  |  MVP pilot · Philippines & Vietnam · 3 months  |  Budget USD 1.5M
Seed Insight

"Nagoya's failure was unmanufactured demand, not price. Doha must make SEA fans want the Games through creators, mother-tongue countdowns, and financial on-ramps."

Creative Brief · anchored to audience & moment

For Feed-Native Fans (SEA Gen-Z, PH & VN), whose job is to discover an event worth wanting — and prove they were there, at the final 30-day countdown — our campaign will put local-language Creator Squads and a one-tap layaway in their feed, so they become first-time ticket buyers.

The Big Idea

FIRST WHISTLE
"We don't wait for fans. We create them."
A creator-led demand engine: national Creator Squads ignite want, a 5-language daily countdown converts it in the final 30 days, "My First Games" layaway with a transparent all-in total removes the money barrier, and every ticket becomes a shareable "I Was There" identity.

Positioning

For SEA's mobile-first Gen-Z and first-time eventgoers who have never been given a reason to care about an Asian Games abroad, FIRST WHISTLE is the demand-creation campaign that makes Doha 2030 the event their feed is already talking about — because it is built on the creators they trust, in the language they speak, with a price they can pay over time. Unlike official host advertising, it manufactures want market by market instead of waiting for it.

Target Segments

Feed-Native Fans (Gen-Z, PH & VN)First-Game FamiliesSport Diehards (badminton / takraw / basketball)

Offering & Mix (4Ps)

  • Product: the campaign itself is the demand engine — Creator Squads (100 creators: 5 headliners + 45 mid-tier per market), a 5-language countdown content engine, "My First Games" layaway ticketing, and an "I Was There" shareable badge.
  • Price: layaway installments from $12/month; one transparent all-in total (ticket + fees from $48) shown before any click — no hidden charges.
  • Place: TikTok / YouTube Shorts / Facebook feeds in PH & VN; conversion on a mobile-first countdown microsite with one-tap layaway checkout.
  • Promotion: daily mother-tongue countdown content, escalating to the final 30 days; creator-led "your first Games" explainers; badge sharing turns every buyer into a broadcaster.

Pilot Experiment

  • Hypothesis: If Creator Squads + a 5-language countdown engine + layaway checkout run in PH & VN, countdown conversion reaches ≥2.5% and ≥50K paid pilot tickets result — proving the trajectory to 400K SEA tickets at scale.
  • Treatment: 100 creators (2 national squads), daily Tagalog & Vietnamese countdown content, My First Games layaway checkout, "I Was There" badge layer.
  • Control: Held-out baseline market (Malaysia, organic only) for incremental lift measurement.
  • Measurement setup: UTM + creator-level attribution dashboards; layaway funnel tracked weekly; hold→paid cohort analysis; mid-point review at week 6.

Scenario · Campaign Moments (time · place · event)

Moment 1 · T-90 to T-31 days
Creator feeds (TikTok / Shorts) — Creator Squads drop the first countdown series: "your first Games starts here."
Moment 2 · Final 30 days
Phone, local-language feed — daily countdown videos escalate; the layaway link is one tap away.
Moment 3 · Games week
Doha venues — buyers share the "I Was There" badge from the stands; every attendee becomes media.
