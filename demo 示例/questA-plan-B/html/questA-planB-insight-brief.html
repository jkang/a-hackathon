Scout Summary

  • Hangzhou 2023 sold 3.05M tickets (¥610M revenue) at 92% attendance — proof a full stadium is possible when demand is built for years.
  • Nagoya 2026 planned 2.75M tickets but sold only ~605K by T-14 days (22%) — the want was never created.
  • Doha 2030 holds visa-free entry for SEA passports and a Qatar Airways / Hamad Int'l stopover hub — but zero awareness in SEA today.

Org / IP Profile

  • Assets: visa-free SEA entry · Qatar Airways stopover hub · 39 venues / 54 sports · National Vision 2030 tourism KPI · 18-month runway (May 2029 – Oct 2030).
  • Constraints: MVP budget USD 1.5M (5% of USD 30M) · SEA market never activated by an Asian Games host · demand does not exist yet.

Market Trends

SEA fandom is creator-led
Fans discover and trust events through creators, not official channels; official ads alone cannot create want.
Mother-tongue content converts
Local-language countdown content outperforms English brand films in the final 30 days before a decision.
First-time eventgoers need financial on-ramps
SEA's young fans can pay over time but not upfront; layaway and transparent total pricing unlock a segment that never buys at full sticker.

Audience Segments — who they are & the job they hire

Feed-Native Fans
SEA Gen-Z, 18–24, mobile-first, Philippines & Vietnam
Job: discover an event worth wanting — and be early to what everyone will talk about (fear of missing a once-in-a-decade moment).
Barrier: zero awareness — nobody has made them care.
Trigger: a local creator's countdown series in their own language.
First-Game Families
Young parents & first-time international eventgoers
Job: attend a major event with no financial surprises — give the family a first Games memory, pride without anxiety.
Barrier: upfront price and hidden fees.
Trigger: layaway plan with a transparent all-in total.
Sport Diehards
Badminton / sepak takraw / basketball followers
Job: watch their sport's biggest stage live — expert status in their fan community, vindication that their sport matters.
Barrier: don't know Doha hosts their sport.
Trigger: sport-specific creator content + session drop alerts.
