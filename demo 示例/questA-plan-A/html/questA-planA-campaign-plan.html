THE AWAY CROWD — organise 680M scattered SEA fans into Doha's loudest away crowd

OCA — Doha 2030 Ticketing & Fan Engagement Unit  |  MVP pilot · Indonesia & Philippines · 3 months  |  Budget USD 1.5M
Seed Insight

"SEA fans don't need convincing that the Games matter — they need a crew, one transparent price, and seats together. Organise the group chat, and you organise the market."

Creative Brief · anchored to audience & moment

For the Chant Leaders, whose emotional job is tribal pride — leading the stand, at the payday-weekend group-chat drop — our campaign will hand captains a one-price Crew Pass link with one-tap split payment, so they recruit their whole crew before the chat goes cold.

The Big Idea

THE AWAY CROWD
"One region. One roar."
Crew Captains recruit fans with a one-price Crew Pass (flight + ticket + stay, visa-free, one-tap e-wallet split); the Away Section seats every crew together; the Roar Ranking turns six countries' pride into a live leaderboard.

Positioning

For Southeast Asian fans who want to experience Doha 2030 together, THE AWAY CROWD is the official away-fan program that removes every friction between the group chat and the grandstand — unlike past hosts that waited for demand, we organise it crew by crew.

Target Segments

The Chant LeadersThe First-StampersThe Flag Carriers

Offering & Mix (4Ps)

  • Product: Crew Pass bundle (flight + ticket + stay, one final price), Away Section seat blocks, Crew ID + Roar Ranking.
  • Price: transparent bundles from USD 399; one-tap split via GoPay / GCash; captain rebates on every recruited seat.
  • Place: official microsite + e-wallet mini-programs + captain storefronts; Qatar Airways co-op inventory.
  • Promotion: 50 Crew Captains, hero film "Who's In?", payday-timed drops in Bahasa, Tagalog & English.

Pilot Experiment

  • Hypothesis: if captain-led recruitment + one-price bundles run in ID & PH for 3 months, ticket-hold conversion reaches ≥1.6% and crew share ≥35% — a trajectory that scales to 400K SEA tickets.
  • Treatment: 50 Crew Captains + Crew Pass bundles (Qatar Airways co-op) + Roar Ranking + payday-timed drops, in Indonesia & the Philippines.
  • Control: Vietnam — official organic channels only; no captains, no bundles.
  • Measurement setup: UTM + captain referral codes + e-wallet payment attribution; weekly funnel readout (reach → visit → crew sign-up → hold → paid); mid-pilot review at week 6.
