Fill the Stands at Doha 2030 — the SEA Away-Crowd Opportunity

Client · OCA — Doha 2030 Ticketing & Fan Engagement Unit  |  Quest A · Plan A  |  Data as of 2026-09

Scout Summary

  • Hangzhou 2023 proved a 92%-full Games is possible: 3.05M tickets, ¥610M revenue.
  • Nagoya 2026 planned 2.75M and sold only ~605K by T-14 days (22%) — demand was never manufactured.
  • Doha's edge is unique: visa-free SEA entry, one Qatar Airways hop, 680M mobile-first fans Nagoya left unmined.

Org / IP Profile

  • Assets: visa-free SEA entry (near-zero awareness), Qatar Airways + Hamad Int'l hub, 54 sports at 39 venues, National Vision 2030 tourism KPI, 18-month runway.
  • Constraints: MVP budget USD 1.5M (5% of USD 30M); no host has ever run an organised SEA away-fan program.

Market Trends

Concert tourism is already normalised
SEA fans already fly regionally for 3-day events; a Games is a bigger reason to fly. The behaviour is proven — only the organiser is missing.
Group payments are the social glue
GoPay / GCash / TrueMoney split links decide whether a group chat becomes a group booking; if splitting isn't one tap, the crew dissolves.
Fandom follows athletes, not federations
Badminton, takraw and football stars outdraw the Games brand in SEA feeds; athlete- and creator-led content is the trust layer official ads can't buy.

Audience Segments — who they are & the job they hire

The Chant Leaders
Hardcore SEA club supporters, 20–40 — already organising away days for AFF / club football & badminton
Job: follow the team abroad · earn capo status · feed tribal pride
Barrier: no official away block; nobody organises the Games like a club does
Trigger: an official Away Section allocation + a captain's armband
The First-Stampers
Gen-Z experience collectors, 18–26 — already fly regionally for concerts
Job: a bucket-list trip that fits a first salary · be the friend with the best story
Barrier: visa myth + opaque total cost
Trigger: the words "visa-free" + one transparent bundle price
The Flag Carriers
SEA diaspora in the Gulf — largest: Filipinos in Qatar
Job: watch the national team live · represent home in a foreign city
Barrier: day-off coordination + no one to go with
Trigger: payday + a compatriot crew invite

Market Truths — from the team

We charter buses across borders for AFF away games — nobody has ever chartered us to a Games. The first question in every travel thread is literally: "is Qatar visa-free for us?" K-pop fans fly to Singapore for concerts; the #1 complaint in fan groups is hidden costs, not price. No split-payment link, no group buy — the chat just dies. People follow the player, not the event; one badminton star post beats a month of federation ads. OFWs in the Gulf want to wave the flag at home soil — they need a payday and a crew. Sitting with strangers kills it; a block of our own people is the whole point. A country-vs-country ranking would spread by itself — SEA cannot resist a leaderboard.
