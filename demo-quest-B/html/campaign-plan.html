PANDA PASSPORT — Turn Panda Love into a Global Membership

Chengdu Research Base of Giant Panda Breeding — Global Brand & IP Division  |  MVP pilot · Japan + Indonesia · 3 months  |  Budget USD 1.1M
Seed Insight

"Millions already love pandas like their own, yet there's no way to belong from afar — so turn that love into a global membership, at the moment a panda clip goes viral."

Creative Brief · anchored to audience & moment

For Panda Parents, whose emotional need for protectiveness, at the moment a panda clip goes viral — our campaign will offer founding membership, so they adopt and belong.

The Big Idea

PANDA PASSPORT
One bear. One world. One membership.
A global membership: adopt a panda, get live cams, member-only content and a welcome kit. Founding 100,000 offer — the first 100,000 get a numbered passport, adoption certificate and welcome kit at $30/yr, with a 'Founder' badge for life.

Positioning

For panda lovers worldwide who watch from afar, PANDA PASSPORT is a global membership that lets you belong, not just visit. Unlike a zoo ticket, it turns fandom into an ongoing relationship and measurable conservation impact.

Target Segments

Panda ParentsGlobal collectorsWholesome families

Offering & Mix (4Ps)

  • Product: Membership: Free / Founding $30/yr / Patron $120/yr
  • Price: Tiered annual membership + licensed merch
  • Place: TikTok / IG / YouTube, licensed retail, telco & ecommerce
  • Promotion: Founding 100,000 launch; localized content; 24/7 live cams

Pilot Experiment

  • Hypothesis: Founding 100,000 with localized content + live cams in Japan & Indonesia reaches member conversion ≥3% and CAC ≤$8 — predicting the 1M-member goal.
  • Treatment: Founding-member campaign: live cams, adoption certificates, localized content, global-store soft launch (JP & ID)
  • Control: Held-out baseline market: Brazil (public content only)
  • Measurement setup: Sign-up funnel + UTM weekly; creator dashboards; merch sell-through; member cohort tracker; mid-point review at T-6 weeks
