1. Short Opening / 简短开场

English

Welcome, everyone.
Today, we will work with AI to co-create a ticketing and fan engagement campaign idea for the Doha 2030 Asian Games and turn it into a clear business proposal.
AI will help us research, benchmark, develop options and produce the campaign materials. The team will make the key strategic and creative decisions.
Our challenge is to turn Asia’s sports enthusiasm into stadium attendance. We will select two Asian pilot markets and develop a three-month pilot (with a $1.5M budget) that can prove how to fill the stands and pave the way to bringing 400,000 Asian fans to Doha.
At the end, we will bring the complete idea together in a five-minute Showcase and present it to the decision-makers.

中文

欢迎大家。
今天，我们会与 AI 一起为 Doha 2030 亚运会共创一个门票与球迷互动（Ticketing & Fan Engagement）的 Campaign idea，并把它发展成一份清晰的商业提案。
AI 会帮助我们完成对标研究、生成选项和制作 Campaign 物料。团队负责作出关键的战略与创意决策。
我们的挑战，是把全亚洲对体育的热情转化为多哈赛场的上座率。我们将选择两个亚洲试点市场，并设计一个预算为 150 万美元的三个月试点计划，用来验证如何填满看台，并为最终吸引 40 万亚洲球迷赴多哈观赛打下基础。
最后，我们会把完整方案整合成一个五分钟的 Showcase，向决策者进行展示。

Facilitator note / 主持提示：Keep this opening to approximately 45 seconds. / 将开场控制在约 45 秒。

2. Short Process Introduction / 简短流程介绍

English

We will move through four steps: Insight, Plan, Media Kit and Showcase.
First, Insight. AI will research the Hangzhou 2023 success and Nagoya 2026 lessons, analyze audience segments, and present insights. We will select the priority Asian audiences and strategic insights that give the campaign the strongest foundation.
Second, Plan. We will turn those choices into a campaign concept, offer, channel approach, two pilot markets, and a three-month pilot plan with benchmark-backed metrics.
Third, Media Kit. AI will create campaign poster options and a storyboard. We will select the poster direction that communicates the idea most clearly.
Finally, Showcase. We will organise the proposal into a clear presentation, select the storyline and decide whether an additional media element will strengthen the pitch.
At each decision point, review the options, discuss the evidence and select the combination that best supports the campaign.

中文

整个流程包括四个步骤：Insight、Plan、Media Kit 和 Showcase。
第一步，Insight。 AI 会研究杭州 2023 的成功经验与名古屋 2026 的教训，分析受众并提供洞察选项。我们会选择能够为 Campaign 建立最强基础的亚洲目标受众和战略洞察。
第二步，Plan。 我们会把这些选择发展成 Campaign concept、Offer、渠道方案、两个试点市场以及基于数据测算的三个月试点计划。
第三步，Media Kit。 AI 会生成 Campaign Poster 选项和 Storyboard。我们会选择最能清楚表达 Campaign idea 的 Poster 方向。
最后，Showcase。 我们会把方案整理成一个清晰的演示故事，选择 Storyline，并决定是否增加一个媒体元素来强化 Pitch。
在每一个决策点，请查看选项、讨论证据，并选择最能支持 Campaign 的组合。

Facilitator note / 主持提示：Keep this process introduction to approximately 60 seconds. Point to the four steps as you name them. / 将流程介绍控制在约 60 秒，并在介绍时指出四个步骤。

What happens / 这一步在做什么

How the Skills Work / Skills 如何运作

English — facilitator script

You may notice that one short instruction can trigger several connected actions. This is possible because the toolkit contains a set of predefined Skills.
For Quest A, the Insight Skill guides AI through benchmark analysis (Hangzhou vs Nagoya), Asian audience segmentation, and Key Insight development. It presents the relevant choices, waits for the team's decisions and records the selected results in the Insight Brief.
Prompt starts the work, Skills provide the method, and the team provides strategic judgment.

中文参考

大家可能会注意到，我们只输入一条简短指令，AI 就会连续完成多项相互关联的工作。这是因为 Toolkit 中预先定义了一组 Skills。
在 Quest A 中，Insight Skill 会引导 AI 完成杭州与名古屋的对标研究、亚洲受众分群和 Key Insight 提炼。它会展示选项，等待团队决策，并将选中的结果记录进 Insight Brief。
Prompt 用来启动工作，Skills 提供执行方法，团队则在决策点提供判断。

What the team selects / 团队需要选择什么

  1. Two or three audience segments from the AI-generated audience options.

  2. Two or three key insights from the AI-generated insight options.

Quick selection criteria / 快速选择标准

For the audience segments, ask / 选择受众时请判断：

  • Do they have strong passion for specific sports/stars (e.g., badminton, esports, table tennis)? / 他们是否对特定项目或明星（如羽毛球、电竞、乒乓球等）有极高热情？
  • Can Qatar's visa-free and flight connectivity easily convert them? / 卡塔尔免签和航线网络能否顺畅促成他们的出行转化？
  • Is there a realistic path from awareness to ticket/package purchase? / 是否存在从关注到购买门票/打包产品的现实路径？

For the Key Insights, ask / 选择 Key Insights 时请判断：

  • Is it backed by data from Hangzhou or Nagoya benchmarks? / 是否有杭州或名古屋的数据/教训作为支持？
  • Does it explain how to manufacture demand in local Asian markets? / 是否解释了如何在亚洲区域市场中“制造”观赛需求？
  • Does it lead to an actionable campaign concept? / 是否能够导出极具落地的创意概念？

The goal is to prove to OCA and Doha decision-makers that our 5% MVP can unlock the full $30M war chest.

English

中文

Inspiring the team to create bonus media / 引导团队创作加分媒体

English — facilitator script

Bonus media is optional, but one strong element can make the pitch memorable. Treat it as a creative stretch, not extra homework — aim for one bold idea that amplifies the campaign, not a polished production.
To spark ideas, ask each sub-group to pitch one media idea in 60 seconds, always tied to the campaign idea and hashtag: a chant the away crowd would actually sing, an 8-second moment a fan would share, or one image that captures the idea.
Then dot-vote and keep the single strongest idea. Take the ready-made prompt from prompt-pack.html — song → Suno, video → Runway, image → GPT / Kimi — generate it, bring the file back, and the AI embeds it into the deck.
Keep it time-boxed: one format, one idea, five minutes to generate, then rehearse with it. If it does not land, drop it — the business case stands on its own.

中文参考

加分媒体是可选项，但一个有力的元素能让 Pitch 更令人记住。把它当作一次创意发挥，而不是额外作业——目标是有一个响亮的好点子来强化 Campaign，而不是做一场精致的制作。
激发灵感：请每个小组用 60 秒提出一个媒体点子，并且必须紧扣 Campaign 的核心创意与话题标签：客队球迷真的会唱出来的助威口号、一个球迷愿意分享的 8 秒瞬间，或一张能表达创意核心的图片。
然后用点投票留下唯一最强的点子。从 prompt-pack.html 里取出现成的提示词——歌曲 → Suno，视频 → Runway，图片 → GPT / Kimi——生成后把文件带回，由 AI 嵌入 deck。
控制时间：一种形式、一个点子、五分钟生成，然后带着它彩排。如果效果不佳就放弃——商业方案本身已经成立。

Facilitator note / 主持提示：Keep this step to about 5 minutes and move the team toward a decision; a single well-chosen element beats several unfinished ones. / 将这一步控制在约 5 分钟，并推动团队尽快做出决定；一个选得准的元素胜过多条没做完的内容。

Bilingual Reference Appendix (Quest A Specifics)

A. Quest A Audience Candidates Examples / 亚洲受众候选参考

  1. Die-hard Niche Sports Fanatic (e.g., Badminton / Kabaddi / Esports)
    Fans whose favorite sports sold out instantly in Nagoya, willing to travel anywhere to support national stars.

小众强项死忠粉（羽毛球/卡巴迪/电竞等）：在名古屋迅速售罄的项目粉丝，愿意为了国家队明星飞赴任何赛场。

  1. The "Qatar Stopover" Global Asian Traveler
    Mobile-first Asian travelers taking international trips who can easily add a 2-day Doha Asian Games experience to their itinerary.

“过境多哈”的亚洲跨国游客：习惯中转旅行的年轻受众，极易被“过境免签+看一场顶级赛事”的打卡 Bundle 吸引。

  1. Diaspora & Expat Fan Communities in the Gulf (GCC)
    South/Southeast Asian professionals living in/near Qatar, ready to organize massive "away crowds" for their home nations.

海湾地区（GCC）的亚洲侨民与外派群体：生活在卡塔尔及周边国家的南亚/东南亚人群，能迅速被激活形成庞大的“客队方阵”。

  1. Gen-Z Sports & Cultural Creators / Fan Club Leaders
    KOLs and fan community organizers who can package travel + ticket deals directly to tens of thousands of followers.

Z 世代体育/文化社群领袖：具备自媒体号召力的球迷会会长及 KOL，能够直接带货“机酒门票套餐”。

B. Quest A Key Insight Examples / 洞察参考示例

Insight 1 — Demand Doesn't Appear by Itself; It Must Be Manufactured Local-by-Local

Nagoya left Asia's mobile-first youth unmined by relying on traditional ticketing, resulting in <30% sold rates, whereas Hangzhou actively built regional sponsor and merch pipelines.
Campaign Implication: Build market-specific bundled packages (Flight + Hotel + Guaranteed Match Ticket) pushed through local mobile-first channels.

Insight 2 — The "Away Crowd" Craves National Pride & Belonging in Overseas Arenas

Asian sports fans traveling abroad don't just buy a ticket; they buy a community experience to chant and showcase national pride in an international venue.
Campaign Implication: Sell "Away Team Fan Zones" with exclusive national fan kits, flag-waving rituals, and group seating.

Insight 3 — Frictionless Transit Unlocks Spontaneous Fan Trips

Qatar's visa-free policy and Qatar Airways hub network eliminate traditional travel friction, yet fans aren't aware how easy a 48-hour Asian Games stopover can be.
Campaign Implication: Launch a 1-click "Asian Games Transit Pass" targeting stopover travelers at Hamad International Airport.
