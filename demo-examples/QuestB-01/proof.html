3 · How each number is derived

Creator reach (6 weeks)
50M
Japan's panda-less void (NHK 2025-12-15) + panda-monium earning billions of views → a 6-week JP+KR creator push reaches ~40% of an addressed fan base of ~125M → sum of campaign impressions (organic + paid)
Free → Guardian conversion
2.2%
Patreon free-to-paid average ~2% (best-in-class ~10%) — Okoa 2024 → a named-panda offer + live panda-cam converts around the Patreon average → paying Guardians / registered Keepers
Paying Guardians (3 months)
25,000
He Hua photobook >100K copies; Fu Bao 2.7M goods; 250K Ueno debut applications → JP+KR converts 2.2% of a ~1.14M free Keeper base → Keepers × 2.2%
Blended CAC per Guardian
≤ $13
Facebook arts/entertainment CPL USD 18.17; Google Events USD 26.84 (Venuera 2026-07) → creator-led organic + member referral cuts paid dependency ~30% → paid media spend / paying Guardians
Member drop / merch attach
30%
Fu Bao 2.7M goods; Seoul pop-up >₩1B and sold out in 5 min (Hankook Ilbo 2024) → 3 in 10 Guardians buy at least one limited drop → Guardians with ≥1 purchase / total Guardians
