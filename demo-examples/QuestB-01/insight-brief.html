Scout Summary

  • The asset: 200+ giant pandas — the world's largest conservation & breeding population, ¥55 entry; weekday 15–20K visitors/day, summer 30–42K, weekend peaks 45K (capacity ceiling).
  • The gap: fame is global, monetization is local — no membership program, no global store, no English-first content engine; international visitors are only ~5% of footfall.
  • The proof: 487K international visitors in 2025 (+70.7% YoY, Xinhua); one visit to see He Hua ≈ ¥1,500 city-wide spend (Qunar); Fu Bao merch 2.7M units in Korea (+60%).

Org / IP Profile

  • Assets: 200+ pandas incl. global stars He Hua & Fu Bao; world's largest conservation & breeding population; Chengdu = China's #2 attraction; visa-free access for inbound fans (77 countries, 240-hr transit); 18-month runway + USD 11M approved.
  • Constraints: conservation-first brand values — no over-commercialization; MVP budget USD 1.1M (10% of USD 11M), 3 months, 2 overseas markets; panda leases/access are politically sensitive; IUCN "Vulnerable".
