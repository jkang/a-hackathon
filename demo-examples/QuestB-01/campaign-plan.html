The reach the Base never owned becomes an owned, paying community.

Positioning

For devoted panda fans and the creators who already make panda content, who want their bond with Hua Hua & Fu Bao to keep going — and to be rewarded for the content they make, Panda Studio is an open AI short-video competition and membership that turns fandom into a paid, always-on bond. Unlike fan accounts and third-party merch, we own the funnel, pay the creators, and keep Hua Hua & Fu Bao at the centre.

Target Segments

The Devoted 福宝/香香 GenerationPanda Creators & UGC Accounts

Channel & Moment

  • Primary: Short-video creator platforms — TikTok / YouTube / Douyin (the competition runs where the fandom already lives).
  • Support: JP/KR super-fan channels — X, LINE, Naver/KakaoTalk (the anniversary livestream); community + CRM — email, WeChat, Xiaohongshu (membership & drops); OTA/travel partners (the "meet the stars" trip).
  • Why: Insight 4 — creators already generate the reach but own none of it; Insight 2 — the fandom peaks at the anniversary livestream with no funnel. A creator competition on short-video owns the reach and routes the peak to a membership.
  • Moment: the monthly creator drop + the anniversary premiere livestream.

Offering & Mix (4Ps)

  • Product: an always-on AI short-video studio + competition (creators star Hua Hua & Fu Bao) and the Studio Membership — behind-the-scenes, members-only premieres, a vote, a numbered seasonal drop.
  • Price: Studio Membership $29/yr (founders $19 first year); the Creator Pass is free with a 20% revenue share; premiere drops $19 each.
  • Place: official web/app + YouTube/TikTok/Douyin channels; the D2F shop; travel partners (OTA) for the "meet the stars" trip; licensed retail.
  • Promotion: monthly competition cycle → anniversary premiere livestream → membership CTA; creator seeding and member referral.

The Offer

A global AI short-video studio where creators star Hua Hua & Fu Bao and share the revenue — and fans join a membership to watch, vote and belong.

  • Create · the Studio Pass: creators get the official Hua Hua/Fu Bao footage kit + AI tooling, enter the monthly competition, and keep a 20% revenue share on every membership and merch sale their film drives.
  • Belong · the Studio Membership ($29/yr): behind-the-scenes, members-only premieres, a vote on the winning film, and a numbered seasonal drop.
  • Collect · the Premiere Drops ($19): a numbered AI film drop each cycle; the winning film ships as a licensed collectible + merch line.

Pilot Experiment

  • Hypothesis: If we launch an AI short-video competition starring Hua Hua & Fu Bao with a revenue share, and route the anniversary livestream to a paid Studio Membership in Japan and South Korea, then creator submissions and membership sign-ups will beat a no-campaign baseline — proving panda love converts into an owned, paying global membership.
  • Treatment: both markets get the localized Panda Studio competition, the official Hua Hua/Fu Bao footage kit, the anniversary premiere livestream, and the membership checkout.
  • Control: a held-out market (Indonesia / SEA) with no campaign, plus the prior-year baseline for the same window.
  • Measurement setup: instrument the studio funnel (submission → watch → vote → sign-up → pay) per market; a weekly dashboard; a mid-point (6-week) check.
