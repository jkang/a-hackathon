PANDA GUARDIANS — a global conservation membership that lets the world belong, not just watch.

Chengdu Research Base of Giant Panda Breeding — Global Brand & IP Division  |  MVP pilot · Japan + South Korea · 3 months  |  Budget USD 1.1M
Seed Insight

"Panda love is global and free; panda belonging is neither — Japan just lost its pandas and Chengdu is now 30-day visa-free, so the fandom is ready to be recruited, Guardian by Guardian."

Creative Brief · anchored to audience & moment

For Japan Panda Orphans & the Fu Bao Diaspora, whose lifelong bond to the pandas now has no local outlet, at the first panda-less Lunar New Year and Fu Bao's return anniversary — our campaign will launch a paid global conservation membership that adopts them a named panda, streams it live and funds its care, so they become a Guardian who belongs from anywhere.

Seoul + global, online & pop-up — Fu Bao's return anniversary: a global live stream + limited drop becomes the membership drive.

The Big Idea

Panda Guardians
"Belong to the pandas. From anywhere."
A global conservation membership: adopt a named Chengdu panda, follow it live on an always-on panda-cam, receive member-only drops, and earn a Chengdu pilgrimage — every dollar funds conservation and is published back to members.

Positioning

For overseas panda fans who have lost their local panda and have no way to belong, Panda Guardians is the official Chengdu Base membership that turns global fandom into conservation funding — unlike a zoo ticket or unlicensed merch, it is the pandas' own home, live and accountable.

Target Segments

Japan Panda OrphansFu Bao DiasporaKawaii Collectors

Offering & Mix (4Ps)

  • Product: 3-tier digital membership — Keeper (free) / Guardian USD 9·mo / Patron USD 25·mo — with panda-cam, named-panda adoption, monthly impact report, member drops, pilgrimage perk.
  • Price: freemium; USD 9/mo core, USD 25/mo patron, annual discount; a published conservation share per tier.
  • Place: owned PWA/app + TikTok / IG / YouTube + local creator squads + Tokyo & Seoul pop-ups.
  • Promotion: creator-led content, serialized panda-cam story arcs, Fu Bao Day (Apr 3), limited-edition drops.

Pilot Experiment

  • Hypothesis: overseas panda fans will pay a recurring membership to belong to a named panda and fund its conservation.
  • Treatment: JP+KR creator squads + Guardian panda-cam + paid membership (2 paid tiers) + the Apr 3 Fu Bao drop.
  • Control: matched JP/KR fan cohorts shown Base content without the membership offer (organic baseline).
  • Measurement setup: one global member ID across web & social; cohort conversion, CAC, 90-day renewal intent, merch attach.

Variant BParked Travel-membership bundle — premium experience & pilgrimage

The Big Idea

Chengdu Pilgrim Pass
"Go to the source. Become a Guardian."
A premium travel-membership bundle — flights + hotel + VIP Base access + a founding Guardian year — sold to high-intent travellers who want the once-in-a-lifetime panda pilgrimage, with membership embedded in the trip.

Positioning

For high-intent inbound travellers and collectors who want the definitive panda trip, Chengdu Pilgrim Pass is the official Base pilgrimage bundle + founding membership — unlike a DIY tour, it guarantees VIP access and lifelong membership status.

Target Segments

Panda-Pilgrim TravelersKawaii CollectorsGlobal Zoo Members

Offering & Mix (4Ps)

  • Product: pilgrimage package (air + hotel + VIP Base access + keeper-for-a-day + 1-yr Guardian) and a founding-members club with a numbered passport.
  • Price: premium package from USD 1,800 pp; founding tier USD 199 with limited seats.
  • Place: travel partners, airline co-marketing, concierge desk, airport/at-gate.
  • Promotion: scarcity (limited founding seats), documentary content, alumni referral.

Pilot Experiment

  • Hypothesis: a premium pilgrimage bundle converts high-intent travellers into paying, high-retention Guardians.
  • Treatment: bundled package live in JP/KR with founding memberships; control = same travellers offered ticket-only.
  • Control: ticket-only booking baseline without the bundle or membership.
  • Measurement setup: booked bundles, attach rate, revenue per traveller, membership activation & renewal.
