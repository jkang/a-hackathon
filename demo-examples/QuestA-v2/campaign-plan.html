Variant AChosen Creator-led crews + Supporter Passport (community-led)

The Big Idea

THE AWAY END
"Book with your crew. Arrive as a nation."
Creator-led national supporter clubs turn scattered Asia fandom into organised away crews — a Supporter Passport unlocks priority tickets, and a Qatar Airways fly-stay bundle removes visa, cost and logistics friction.

Positioning

For Asia Gen-Z fans who want a once-in-a-lifetime trip with their crew, Doha 2030 is the visa-free, close-haul Games — and unlike Nagoya, we recruit supporters into visible national away crews instead of waiting for demand.

Target Segments

The Away CrewThe Gulf CrewThe Family Stand

Offering & Mix (4Ps)

  • Product: Supporter Passport + crew fly-stay-ticket bundle
  • Price: free tier + paid passport; bundle at transparent, tiered pricing
  • Place: TikTok/IG creator feeds · crew group chats · Qatar Airways + Discover Qatar · diaspora clubs
  • Promotion: creator-led recruitment in 4 waves; hero film; countdown; crew referral

Pilot Experiment

  • Hypothesis: If creator-led crews + a Supporter Passport + fly-stay bundles run in ID & TH for 3 months, then crew sign-up ≥3.0% and group-booking ratio ≥35% — enough to predict 400K tickets at scale.
  • Treatment: 20–25 national creators + Supporter Passport + Fly Together bundles (ID & TH)
  • Control: Malaysia — matched, visa-free, organic reach only (no paid crew programme)
  • Measurement setup: UTM + crew-signup + ticket-hold tracking, weekly; creator dashboards; mid-point review at T-6 weeks
