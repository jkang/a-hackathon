From Zoo Visit to World Membership

Chengdu Research Base of Giant Panda Breeding — Global Brand & IP Division  |  MVP pilot · 2 overseas markets · 3 months  |  Budget USD 1.1M (10% of USD 11M)
Seed Insight

“The panda is the world's classic character IP and its most-watched animal — a 50-year icon loved on a permanent, always-on live feed — yet the Base gives that affection nowhere to belong and no way to pay. Turn ambient panda love into a membership.”

Creative Brief · the campaign kernel (audience · moment · trend · channel · concept)

For Kawaii / Blind-Box Collectors (with Panda-Pilgrim inbound travelers), whose emotional job is to own a piece of the world's most-loved animal, at the always-on panda cam and the numbered drop day, riding classic-IP licensing ($161.8B; 77% classic) and drop-collectible culture (Pop Mart +184.7%), our campaign intercepts via short-video + collector creators, and OTA travel funnels with a numbered, provenance-backed panda drop and a travel-linked Panda Pass — so collectors can own and belong, and live-cam viewers can join before they ever book.

The Big Idea

The Numbered Drop
The only panda with a serial number.
The panda is a 50-year classic IP, and collectors pay for provenance — so give the world's most-loved animal a numbered, certified drop series it can own and trade, wrapped in a cause-first membership.

Positioning

For kawaii and blind-box collectors who collect rare cute IP, the Panda Numbered Drop is a collectible membership that ships numbered, globally available panda pieces with provenance. Unlike generic zoo souvenirs, we release a certified, limited series designed to be collected and traded.

Target Segments

Kawaii / Blind-Box CollectorsSEA Short-Video SuperfansCorporate / Gift Buyers

Channel & Moment

  • Primary: TikTok + collector creators (creator-seeded drops)
  • Support: Instagram + Xiaohongshu · Direct-to-fan store (TikTok Shop / Shopify)
  • Why: Drop culture compounds (Pop Mart +184.7%) and cute IP spreads creator-to-creator (creator economy → ~$480B by 2027), so discovery-to-checkout happens on short-video.
  • Moment: the numbered drop day

Offering & Mix (4Ps)

  • Product: Limited, numbered panda collectible drops + a Founding membership
  • Price: $30/yr Founding membership; numbered drops $25–60 (under idol fan clubs ~$40 and zoo memberships $50–120)
  • Place: Direct-to-fan e-commerce (TikTok Shop / Shopify) + partner retail crossover
  • Promotion: Creator-seeded countdown → drop-day livestream → UGC shelfies

The Offer

A numbered panda you can own — join the Founding cohort and get every drop before the public.

  • The Offer: A Founding membership ($30/yr) with first access to every numbered panda drop, plus members-only cam and creator content.
  • Markets: Indonesia + the United States — a proven short-video collector market and the world's largest licensing market.
  • Access: A numbered certificate + provenance per piece; direct-to-fan shipping; a tradeable collector community.

Pilot Experiment · Indonesia + United States

  • Hypothesis: If we launch a creator-seeded numbered drop in Indonesia and the US, then collector sign-ups and drop sell-through will beat a no-drop baseline — predicting that panda fandom converts to paid membership globally.
  • Treatment: Both markets get a creator-seeded numbered drop, a Founding membership, a D2F store, and localized content.
  • Control: Held-out comparison: organic live-cam traffic + the existing ¥55 gate (no drop, no membership); plus the prior-period merch benchmark.
  • Measurement setup: Instrument the D2F funnel (view → sign-up → first drop) end to end; a weekly dashboard; drop sell-through tracked per SKU; 3-month window with a mid-point check at week 6.

The Big Idea

The Pre-Trip Gate Membership
Watch. Join. Then come meet them.
iPanda's ~1B views create always-on panda love — give viewers a membership to join now, and bundle it into the Chengdu trip at the moment of booking, so affection becomes belonging before arrival.

Positioning

For inbound China travelers and live-cam fans who already love the pandas, the Panda Pass is a travel-linked membership that turns viewing into belonging and a guided visit. Unlike a one-off ¥55 gate ticket, we convert affection into a member before arrival and reward the visit.

Target Segments

Panda-Pilgrim Inbound TravelersKawaii / Blind-Box CollectorsJapan's Panda-Less Fanbase

Channel & Moment

  • Primary: OTA & travel partners (Trip.com / Ctrip, airlines) — the booking funnel
  • Support: Live-cam / YouTube content engine · TikTok travel + WeChat / CRM
  • Why: Discovery and payment already live on OTAs and short-video (30.08M visa-free entries, +49.5%), and live cams create ambient fandom (>40% overseas) — so the message converts where the trip is booked.
  • Moment: the visa-free trip-planning / booking window

Offering & Mix (4Ps)

  • Product: Panda Pass membership + a guided 'panda trip' bundle
  • Price: $30/yr Panda Pass; trip bundle attach at the point of booking
  • Place: OTA / airline partner funnels + the Base gate (distribution)
  • Promotion: Always-on live-cam content → pre-trip conversion → a member arrival moment

The Offer

Join before you go — a Panda Pass that turns watching the cam into a member's welcome when you arrive.

  • The Offer: Panda Pass ($30/yr): live-cam + behind-the-scenes content, member pricing on the trip, and a welcome experience at the gate.
  • Markets: Japan + Thailand — a proven panda-affinity market now panda-less, and a high-volume SEA travel market.
  • Access: One-tap join inside the OTA booking flow; membership recognized on arrival; invite-a-friend referral.

Pilot Experiment · Japan + Thailand

  • Hypothesis: If we bundle a Panda Pass into the OTA booking flow in Japan and Thailand, then member sign-ups per 1,000 bookings and pre-arrival conversion will beat a no-bundle baseline — predicting that inbound travelers become paying global members.
  • Treatment: Both markets get a Panda Pass inside the OTA booking flow, localized live-cam content, and a member welcome at the gate.
  • Control: Held-out comparison: OTA bookings without the Pass offer, plus the Base's current gate-only baseline.
  • Measurement setup: Tag the OTA booking funnel (view → Pass attach → arrival check-in); a weekly dashboard; Pass attach rate per market; 3-month window with a mid-point check at week 6.
