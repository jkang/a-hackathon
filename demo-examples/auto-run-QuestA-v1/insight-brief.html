Make Southeast Asia Doha’s biggest “away crowd” in Asian Games history.

Client · OCA — Doha 2030 Ticketing & Fan Engagement Unit  |  Quest A  |  Data as of 2026-09

Scout Summary

  • Hangzhou 2022 proved a 92%-full, ¥610M ($84M) Games; Nagoya 2026 sold only ~22% at T-14 — demand was never manufactured.
  • Doha 2030 runs Nov 4–19, 54 sports, 39 venues — visa-free entry and a world-class Qatar Airways / Hamad Int’l hub.
  • SEA is the youngest, most mobile-first fan base in Asia — and Nagoya just left that market unmined.

Org / IP Profile

  • Assets: Visa-free SEA entry · Qatar Airways + Hamad Int’l hub · 54 sports / 39 venues · QNV 2030 tourism KPI (6M visitors/yr by 2030).
  • Constraints: MVP budget USD 1.5M (5% of USD 30M) · 3 months · 2 SEA pilot markets · 5 languages.

Market Trends

SEA outbound is booming
Thailand 13.06M outbound (+11.4% YoY, ~$15.5B spend); Vietnam fastest-growing (+20.4% in 2025); ASEAN headed to 201M arrivals by 2030.
Creator trust > ads in SEA
Short-video is SEA’s discovery + trust layer — creators drive ticket decisions, not banners.
The Gulf is the new aspiration destination
Doha Golden Week bookings +440% YoY; visa-free + Qatar Airways hub removes the #1 booking barrier.

Benchmark

Gold Standard

Hangzhou 2022 — 3.05M tickets, ¥610M ($84M) revenue, 92% attendance, 290K peak day, ¥760M merch.

Cautionary Tale

Nagoya 2026 — 2.75M planned, ~22% sold at T-14 days; football/baseball seats empty.

Your Arena

Doha 2030 — Nov 4–19, 54 sports; SEA is the youngest, most mobile-first fan base in Asia, left unmined.

Market Truths — from the team

Demand does not appear by itself — it must be manufactured market by market. Fans travel as crews and families, not solo — bundles beat single tickets. Visa-free entry + a hub airport removes the #1 booking barrier.
