THE AWAY END — Organise SEA Fandom into a Loud, Visible Away Crowd

OCA — Doha 2030 Ticketing & Fan Engagement Unit  |  MVP pilot · Indonesia + Thailand · 3 months  |  Budget USD 1.5M
Seed Insight

"SEA Gen-Z already travel in crews and follow creators, yet no Asian Games host has ever activated them — so make SEA Doha's loudest away crowd, starting at the moment a creator film drops."

Creative Brief · anchored to audience & moment

For SEA Gen-Z First-Timers, whose need for belonging + awe, at ticket-sale day — our campaign will run a creator-led crew invite, so they book as a crew.

The Big Idea

THE AWAY END
We don't visit. We arrive.
Creator squads recruit national away crews; Qatar Airways bundles remove friction; a branded away section makes the SEA crowd visible and loud.

Positioning

For SEA sports fans who want a once-in-a-lifetime trip with their crew, Doha 2030 is the Asian Games that is affordable and visa-free. Unlike Nagoya, we organise fans into visible away crews and remove travel friction 18 months ahead.

Target Segments

Gen-Z sports fansDiaspora & crewsGroup travellers

Offering & Mix (4Ps)

  • Product: Campaign + crew bundles (ticket + flight + stay)
  • Price: Tiered ticket + transparent bundle pricing
  • Place: Qatar Airways, ticketing partners, TikTok/IG, diaspora clubs
  • Promotion: Teaser → countdown → hero film; 20 creator squads across 2 markets

Pilot Experiment

  • Hypothesis: Creator-led crew recruitment + bundles in ID & TH raise ticket-intent ≥2.5% and group-booking ratio ≥35% — predicting the 400K SEA ticket target.
  • Treatment: 20 creator squads + 'Fly Together' bundles + crew registration (ID & TH)
  • Control: Held-out baseline market: Philippines (organic only)
  • Measurement setup: UTM + ticket-hold tracking weekly; creator dashboards; crew sign-up funnel; mid-point review at T-6 weeks
