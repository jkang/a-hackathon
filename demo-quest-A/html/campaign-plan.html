The Big Idea

THE AWAY END
We don't visit. We arrive.
Creator squads recruit national away crews; airline bundles remove friction; a branded away section makes SEA visible and loud.

Positioning

For SEA sports fans who want a once-in-a-lifetime trip with their crew, Doha 2030 is the affordable, visa-free Games. Unlike Nagoya, we organise fans into visible away crews.

Target Segments

Gen-Z sports fansDiaspora & crewsGroup travellers

Offering & Mix (4Ps)

  • Product: Campaign + crew bundles
  • Price: Tiered + transparent
  • Place: Qatar Airways · TikTok/IG · diaspora clubs
  • Promotion: Teaser → countdown → hero film; 20 creator squads

Pilot Experiment

  • Hypothesis: Creator-led crew recruitment + bundles raise ticket-intent ≥2.5% and group ratio ≥35%.
  • Treatment: 20 creator squads + Fly Together bundles + crew registration (ID & TH)
  • Control: Philippines (organic only)
  • Measurement setup: UTM + hold tracking weekly; creator dashboards; mid-point at T-6 wks.

Bring the whole crew.
